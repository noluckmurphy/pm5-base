Getting it on the TV

  • On the PM5: More Options → Turn Wireless ON. Pair your heart-rate strap to the PM5 so heart rate comes through too.
  • Click Connect and pick the PM5. Only one app can be connected to the PM5 at a time.
  • To put it on the TV, use an HDMI cable, or Chrome's ⋮ menu → Cast → Cast tab, then pick the TV.
  • The top bar hides itself while you row. Move the mouse to bring it back.
